## The Short Version of My Story

I sold my first video game in high school — 10,000 lines of assembly language, written one instruction at a time. I learned C++ at Bell Labs in 1987, earned my computer science degree at Columbia (Dean's List), and I've been a professional programmer ever since — most of it back-end C++, the kind of code nobody sees but everything depends on.

Along the way I founded software companies, designed a UAV for the U.S. military, wrote two technical books and 350+ articles, and built products used by more than 30,000 people.

These days, I teach. Over 1,000 one-on-one sessions and counting.

## How I Teach

We learn by building. You pick a project you actually care about, and we build it together. I'll show you what's going on under the hood — not just which buttons to press, but *why*.

Sessions are relaxed and practical. I'm patient, we go at your pace, and there are no dumb questions. Forty years in, I can smell bad code a mile away — and I'll teach you to spot it too.

## Coding With AI, Without the Bad Habits

I use ChatGPT and Claude every day in my own work. I'll teach you how to direct them, how to review what they produce, and how to catch them when they're confidently wrong. AI makes a good programmer faster — but only if you understand what it's doing. That understanding is what we build together.
