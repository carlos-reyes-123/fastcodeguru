The Fall 2025 Course and Exam Description still governs AP Computer Science A, and it is not the ten-unit Java survey most old review books were written for. Inheritance, polymorphism, and writing recursive methods are out; data collections, File/Scanner, String.split, and tracing recursion are in. The digital Bluebook exam is 42 multiple-choice questions plus four free-response families—methods and control structures, class design, ArrayList, and 2D arrays—and it rewards small correct algorithms, not OOP theater. This piece maps the four units that actually matter, the Java Quick Reference you should keep open, and the compile-clean traps that cost points: substring bounds, forward ArrayList removal, leftover newlines after nextInt(), public void constructors, and max initialized to zero.
