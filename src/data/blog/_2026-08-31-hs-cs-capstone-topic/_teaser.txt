Most capstones I see are already dead by February: a slide deck with “AI,” a marketplace, and a mobile app, an empty repo, then a panicked switch to a Wix site. A real project takes six to twelve months from the first line of code the student can explain, and passion for the problem is the only fuel that survives that stretch. The stack has to match the actual kid—their hours, their laptop, whether they have a Mac, whether they love soldering or hate compilers—not the brochure. This is a field guide for students, parents, and counselors who want the thing to still exist in April: five honest stacks, a scope you can demo in a minute, how to use AI without handing it the steering wheel, and a decision you can make this week.
